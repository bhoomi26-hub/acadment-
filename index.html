Yes. All four featured institutions are officially recognized within the Indian higher education regulatory framework:

  • DPU: Deemed-to-be University under Section 3 of the UGC Act, with approvals from NMC, DCI, and PCI.
  • Amity University: State private university established under state legislature and recognized by UGC and statutory bodies.
  • Manipal University (MAHE): Institution of Eminence Deemed-to-be University recognized by UGC, AICTE, NMC, and PCI.
  • GGSIPU (IPU): State Teaching and Affiliating University established by the Government of NCT of Delhi, recognized by UGC and AICTE.

Entrance exam requirements vary depending upon the discipline and university:

  • Healthcare (MBBS/BDS): Mandatorily requires qualification in NEET-UG with counselling through MCC / respective state authorities.
  • Engineering (B.Tech): Admission may be granted through JEE Main, institutional exams (e.g. MET for Manipal, IPU-CET/JEE for IPU, Amity JEE), or merit in 10+2 PCM.
  • Management (MBA/BBA): MBA programs generally accept CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, or university tests, while BBA pathways often admit based on 10+2 scores or CUET.

An Acadment advisor will review your specific exam scores and identify applicable pathways.
